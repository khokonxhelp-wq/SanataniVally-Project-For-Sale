## 📖 Overview

**SanataniVally** (also referred to as **Neural Mind**) is a full-featured Android application and web project designed to unite the global Sanatani community. It combines real‑time group communication, AI‑powered spiritual knowledge, community feeds, quizzes, and advanced admin tools into a single, cohesive platform.

The project is currently at **version 5.4** and is being offered as a **complete acquisition** – including the Android APK, HTML/PHP source files, and all associated rights (subject to verification and a written agreement).

> **Mission:** To create a trusted, modern, and respectful digital network for Sanatani users – a “digital parliament” where ancient wisdom meets contemporary technology.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎧 Communication
- **200‑participant HD audio calls** – crystal‑clear, stable even on slow networks (powered by Agora RTC).
- **WhatsApp‑style group chat** – replies, emoji reactions, @mentions, typing indicators, real‑time messaging.
- **In‑app link viewer** – open any link inside the app; YouTube links auto‑play.

### 🧠 AI & Spiritual Knowledge
- **Sanatani AI Assistant** – GPT‑4o powered, answers from the Bhagavad Gita, Vedas, Upanishads, and Puranas.
- **Interactive MCQ quizzes** – create and run live quizzes on religion and scripture; real‑time results.

### 📱 Community & Social
- **Community feed & posts** – share images, text, and MCQ posts; like, comment, and save.
- **Media gallery & link hub** – all group photos, videos, and URLs automatically organised in a dedicated tab.
- **Verified & Primary Admin badges** – blue tick for verified users, red pulse badge for primary admins.

### ⚙️ Administration
- **Advanced admin panel** – user approval, notification logs, custom links, storage analytics, one‑tap data cleanup.
- **Smart notifications** – in‑app alerts for mentions, likes, comments, group closings, and incoming calls (auto‑cleaned after 24 hours).

### 🎨 Customisation
- **Custom themes & dark mode** – personalise button colours, backgrounds, and chat bubbles.

### 🔐 Security
- **Bangladesh & India phone validation** – secure signup with BD (`01XXXXXXXXX`) and India (`+91XXXXXXXXXX`) numbers.
